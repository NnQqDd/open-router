# Open models with documented Vietnamese support

Checked: **30 October 2026**

**Vietnamese support is required for inclusion.** English support is an additional property checked for each Vietnamese-capable release. This inventory contains public model/checkpoint releases, Vietnamese voice collections, and a traditional open-source synthesizer with documented Vietnamese support.

Rows represent releases or collections, not independent architectures. Mirrors, quantizations, and speaker-only derivatives are generally grouped with their parent release. This is a broad inventory rather than a guarantee that every community fine-tune has been enumerated.

Capabilities were checked against author repositories, model cards, and inference instructions. No audio benchmarks were run; documented support does not establish equal quality across languages.

## Reading the English column

- **Yes:** the publisher explicitly documents English synthesis in the Vietnamese-capable release.
- **Declared / tagged / inherited:** English is claimed, but retention after Vietnamese adaptation is not convincingly evaluated.
- **Limited / code-switching:** English capability is restricted or weak; embedded English words do not establish general English synthesis.
- **Separate checkpoint:** Vietnamese and English use different language-specific weights.
- **Unconfirmed:** English support could not be established for the listed Vietnamese release.

TTS generates speech from text. TTS cloning conditions new spoken text on a reference speaker. Voice conversion changes the speaker of existing audio. License entries distinguish code from weights and retain unresolved licensing conflicts.
